# CANN Project Code of Conduct

1. To build an open and friendly community, contributors and maintainers of this community pledge:
   All participants in the community treat each other with respect, free from harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, level of experience, level of education, social status, nationality, personal appearance, race, and the like.

2. Behaviors that contribute to a positive environment for our community include:

   - Using friendly and inclusive language
   - Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
   - Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
   - Focusing on what is best for the community
   - Showing kindness toward other members

3. Behaviors that participants in this community should not engage in include:

   - Posting sexually explicit or violent content
   - Trolling, inciting, spreading rumors, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political violence
   - Harassing other participants in the community in public or in private.
   - Publishing others' information, such as physical and email addresses, without their explicit authorization
   - Other conduct that could reasonably be considered as a violation of this Code of Conduct.

4. Community project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of "proper conduct" and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any conduct that they deem inappropriate. Community project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, Wiki edits, issues, and any other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and will temporarily or permanently ban any participant whose behavior they deem threatening, offensive, or harmful to community order.
